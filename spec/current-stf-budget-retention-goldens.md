# Whole-map STF goldens for budget-exhausted deferred retention

Scope: fifteen authored ten-block chains,150 complete current-candidate transitions. Authority is
Gray Paper `1fa8cbc7e7ec3e60d09f8e704911a89c6837ce1d` accumulation E/Q, `accseq` whole-report
prefix affordability and actual-gas continuation, final ready/history equations, and the existing
reporting, availability, statistics and guest contracts. Draft35 Thin v1/ark-vrf0.6.0 and vector
pins remain those of the preceding current-profile cases.

Every chain honestly admits A→B→C→D→E through guarantees/availability as in the dependency-wave
cases, including segment-root lookups and canonical prerequisite/lookup unions. Releasing A at
slot7 exposes five priority candidates, but the actual processed prefix determines posterior state.
No ready row is injected into the original. All code, requests and namespaces are explicit original
inputs; earlier and upstream fixtures remain unchanged.

Each literal storage mode has three gas profiles under a300000 block budget:

| Profile | A,C,D,E declared gas | B declared gas | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Serial | 300000 | 300000 | A runs at7; B,C,D,E drain one per empty block8..11 |
| Exact residual fit | 300000 | 299978 | After A costs22, B fits exactly at7; C,D,E drain at8..10 |
| One above residual | 300000 | 299979 | B misses by one despite positive residual gas; it waits until8 |

At7, unprocessed B remains dependency-free in row4; C retains only B, D retains C, and newly
available E retains B,D. Hypothetical Q progress does not clear dependencies of unprocessed reports.
Each later actual prefix removes only its processed package and subtracts that package from the
remaining dependencies. Reports with empty dependency sets remain queued and eligible in later
blocks, even with no new extrinsics. Every package enters C15 exactly once; complete retained
report bytes, row positions, sorted dependency sets, history aging and subsequent drain are checked.

The independent reference computes all signed children, whole maps and Q/prefix/residual-gas traces
before native execution. It calculates literal create/replace/delete, rollback and checkpoint effects,
actual gas, service timestamps, footprint changes, C13 activity, C16 output implications and C3
output-sensitive MMR/header commitments. Across all chains75 reports are processed by75 guests.
The shared driver accepts varying chain lengths; strict expected/actual P/B/O comparison binds the
complete length. Production, dependency pins, ambiguity guards and historical fixtures are unchanged.

Verification uses the registered `current_stf_budget_retention_corpus`, all49 prior dependency-wave
transitions, scoped reference/provenance/harness checks, pinned formatting and inventory. Evidence
belongs under `build/state9-stf-budget-retention/`. These authored cases extend current-profile
coverage without establishing full JAM parity. Gas-limited guest exits, transfer-driven re-entry,
late preimage availability and epoch-spanning execution remain subsequent whole-map work.
